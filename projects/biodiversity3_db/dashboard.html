About

Row

Source. Four survey exports: Scoping_CBC2026, Scoping_Davos, Scoping_IDSSD, Scoping_SRI. Only the aggregated per-question sheets were used, plus participant count and country from the main sheet. The name, username and e-mail columns are never read; the message-wall sheets were ignored.

Participants = rows in each main-results sheet, including people who joined but answered little. Countries are mentions: some people listed two, and answers were normalised (e.g. Scotland and UK → United Kingdom).

Questionnaires differ by event. Questions were matched by wording, not number. CBC 2026 asked about discussion topics but not about engagement or barriers; IDSSD covered both Arctic 3 (A3) and Biodiversity 3 (BD3); SRI’s barrier list had extra options. IDSSD labels the top of its 0–4 scales “3”; because the wording matches, it is plotted as 4. The headline percentages pool Biodiversity 3 responses only (IDSSD’s A3 answers are shown separately).

Small samples. Most questions have 5–16 responses per event. Treat everything as indicative.

Keyword coding. Themes for co-design partners and 30 by 30 factors come from simple keyword rules in build_data.R (CODESIGN_THEMES, FACTORS_THEMES). They are a first pass for orientation; edit the rules or recode by hand.

Empty questions. Knowledge gaps (Davos, SRI), unique value and added component (IDSSD, SRI) and transdisciplinarity → policy (SRI) have no recorded answers.

Rebuild. Put the four .xlsx files in raw/, run Rscript build_data.R raw, then quarto render dashboard.qmd.
